---
title: Fitbit
---

Fitness data from Fitbit devices can be shared with Digital Carrot by installing the Google Health app
and configuring it to share data with Apple Health.

## iPhone

> [!NOTE]
> You don't need to share all of your Apple Health data with Google. Granting Google Health permission
> to only write to Apple Health is sufficient for Digital Carrot.


[Share data with Apple Health](https://lifehacker.com/health/fitbit-data-can-finally-sync-to-apple-health)
